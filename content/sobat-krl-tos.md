**Last Updated: July 13, 2026**

#### 1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using Sobat KRL, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree to these terms, please do not use the application.

#### 2. Offline-First & Data Privacy

Sobat KRL is built with a strict offline-first design philosophy:

- **No Data Collection:** The application operates 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, user data, trip histories, search queries, or device analytics.
- **On-Device Location Processing:** When using the nearest-station finder feature, device GPS coordinates are processed entirely locally on your device via geometric distance calculations. No location coordinates or coordinates history are ever uploaded to a remote server.

#### 3. Data Accuracy Disclaimer

Sobat KRL provides route planning, line lists, and scheduled transit info compiled offline from community registries and Grafik Perjalanan Kereta Api (GAPEKA) timetables:

- **Advisory Use Only:** Transit times, routes, and transfer information are calculated locally and are intended solely for planning and convenience purposes.

- **No Liability:** We are not liable for any delays, missed train connections, route changes, or disruptions to your travel journey. Please cross-reference schedules with official sources or station announcements during active travel.

#### 4. Changes and Updates

We reserve the right to periodically update our offline database, app configurations, and these Terms of Service to improve data accuracy or reflect changes in the commuterline networks.
