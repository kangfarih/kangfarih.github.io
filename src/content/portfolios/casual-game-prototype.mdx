* * *

### Overview

*   Role: Front End Programmer
*   Type: Mobile Game Prototype
*   Technology: Unity
*   Focus: Prototype gameplay and fast iteration

### Role

I worked with game and graphic designers. As the front-end programmer, I implemented the gameplay mechanics and integrated assets into a playable prototype.
